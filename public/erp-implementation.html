ERP implementation that starts with a pilot, not a big bang.

Nastaliq configures accounting, stock, buying, selling and manufacturing around how your business already works. Each stage gives you something working before the next one starts, and most companies are live in about six weeks.

Four stages

  1. A walkthrough. You show us how orders, stock and money move today, and we tell you plainly whether we are a fit.
  2. A pilot. We set up one branch, one warehouse or one process first, and your team runs real work on it.
  3. The move. Records come over, staff learn on their own screens, and the old system stays readable until you are sure.
  4. Day to day. Hosting, updates and support from the people who built it. Your data exports whenever you want it.

What gets set up

  • Chart of accounts and tax reports, with your accountant.
  • Items, warehouses and branches.
  • Customers, suppliers and price lists.
  • Buying, selling and, if you make things, manufacturing.
  • Users, and what each role can see.

Cost

Launch is a one-time $6,000 to $12,000, depending on the size of the setup. After that, one monthly plan from $400, with unlimited users.

FAQ

Implementation questions

How long does ERP implementation take?

About six weeks for a standard launch, from walkthrough to go-live.

Why start with a pilot?

Running real work on one branch, warehouse or process shows problems early, while they are cheap to fix, and gives your team confidence before everything moves.

Do you train our staff?

Yes. Staff learn on their own screens, and training is part of the launch fee.

What happens after go-live?

We host the system, keep it updated and support your team. Your data can be exported whenever you want it.
