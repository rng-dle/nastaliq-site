Common questions

Can we see the profit on each job?

Yes. Costs from timesheets, purchases and stock are tied to the job, so you can compare them with what you have billed.

Can crews use it from a phone?

Yes. Nastaliq runs in a browser on a phone, so crews can record time and stock movements on site.

Yes, as a paid add-on. Data migration is quoted separately from the implementation fee. Tell us what you use today and we will say what is possible.
