What we do

Nastaliq sets up and hosts a private cloud ERP for growing businesses: inventory, purchasing, sales and accounting in one system. Each client runs on its own dedicated server, with its own name and logo, and its data is never shared with another company.

We start from a preset for your industry, bring in your existing records and stay responsible for hosting, updates and support after go-live. Pricing is a flat monthly subscription with unlimited users plus a one-time setup fee.
