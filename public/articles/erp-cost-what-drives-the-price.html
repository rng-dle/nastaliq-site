What drives the cost of an ERP, and what to ask vendors

Buying guide · Published November 12, 2026 · By the Nastaliq team

The short answer

ERP cost is more than the monthly fee. Compare quotes on users, implementation, data migration, hosting, updates and support, and ask what each one includes before you sign.

The number on a quote is rarely the number you pay. These are the parts that move the cost of an ERP, and what to ask about each.

Subscription or licence

Most cloud ERPs charge monthly. Ask what the fee covers: hosting, updates, backups, support. Some vendors charge extra for each of them.

Number of users

Per-user pricing looks cheap for a team of five and expensive for a team of thirty. It also discourages the people on the floor from using the system. A flat fee with unlimited users is easier to plan around.

Implementation

Someone has to configure the system for your business: accounts, taxes, roles, documents, workflows. This is usually a one-time fee. Ask what is included and what counts as extra work.

Data migration

Bringing your customers, products, suppliers and balances across is a separate job from configuration. It depends on how clean your data is and how much history you want. Ask for it as its own line item so you can see what you are paying for.

Hosting

A shared system costs less to run than a dedicated server per client, and the price usually reflects that. If your data must stay private, ask where it lives and who else shares the machine.

Customisation and integrations

Anything beyond the standard system, such as a custom report, a connection to your online store or a tax connector, is typically quoted separately. Get the list in writing.

Support and updates

Ask how fast support responds, who answers and whether updates arrive automatically. Software that is never updated becomes a cost of its own.

How to compare quotes

Put each vendor in the same table: subscription, users, implementation, data migration, hosting, support, extras. Fill every cell, and ask about any that is blank. The cheapest headline is often the most expensive project.

Common questions

Why do ERP quotes differ so much?

Vendors bundle different things. One quote may include implementation, hosting and support while another lists only a licence. Line the quotes up item by item before comparing totals.

Is data migration included in the implementation fee?

Often not. Migration is its own piece of work, and many vendors, including Nastaliq, charge for it separately. Ask for it as a line item.

Does per-user pricing matter?

Yes. A low price per user grows with every hire, and it pushes teams to share logins. Ask whether users are unlimited.
