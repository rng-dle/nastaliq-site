    Services

    What we set up and run

    ERP implementation

    Accounting, stock, buying, selling and manufacturing, configured around your process instead of the other way round.

    Good fit: manufacturers, distributors and retailers with several branches.

    Data migration

    Receipts, delivery notes, handwritten logs, exports from your old software. We bring years of records in, so your history is searchable instead of sitting in a cupboard.

    Good fit: companies moving off paper or legacy systems.

    E-invoicing

    We connect your invoicing to the tax authority, so invoices are reported as they are issued and rejected ones are easy to fix.

    Good fit: companies with e-invoicing deadlines.

    Custom apps

    Point of sale for restaurants and bakeries, morning alerts for low stock and late payments, dashboards, and your own branding on the admin.

    Good fit: teams that need more than the standard modules.

    FAQ

    Questions we hear first

    What am I paying for?

    Setup, hosting, updates and support for your team. There is no software licence fee and no per-user fee.

    Can we leave and take our data with us?

    Yes. Your system runs on open-source software, so your data and setup can move to any other host or partner.

    Where is our data hosted?

    On managed cloud hosting in the region closest to you, with offsite backups.

    We already have a system like this. Can you take it over?

    Yes. We start by reviewing your customisations and fix anything fragile before adding new work.

    Which e-invoicing systems do you connect to?

    We build each country's connection as clients need it. Tell us where you invoice and we will tell you what it takes.

    Do you work with our accountant?

    Yes. We set up the chart of accounts and tax reports with them.
